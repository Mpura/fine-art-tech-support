⚡ Laser Cutter File Preparation Guide

Fine Art Tech Support — Rhodes University Fine Art Department

Read this guide before your session. Correct file setup avoids wasted time and re-cuts.

Step 1 — Set up your Illustrator file

1
Document size
Set your artboard to match the laser bed. Keep your design within the printable area and leave at least 6mm margin from all edges.
2
Colour mode: RGB
Go to File → Document Colour Mode → RGB Colour. CMYK files will not drive the laser correctly.
3
Stroke weight: hairline
All vector cut/engrave lines must have a stroke weight of 0.001–0.005pt with NO fill. Thicker strokes are treated as raster etches.

Step 2 — Colour-code your layers

The laser reads specific RGB colours to decide what to do. Use separate layers for each operation.

ColourRGB ValueOperationSpeed
Red255, 0, 0Vector Cut — cuts all the way throughFast
Blue0, 0, 255Vector Engrave — scores the surfaceFast
Black0, 0, 0Raster Etch — detailed shading/imagesSlow ⚠️
💡 Tip: Engrave (etch) first, then cut. In Illustrator, place your etch layer above your cut layer so it runs first.
⚠️ Raster etching takes much longer than vector cutting. A job with heavy etching may need a 2-hour session.

Step 3 — Know your material

Maximum cut depth: 12mm. Materials thicker than this cannot be cut through.

Be aware of material warping — wood, card and some plastics warp, which can cause inaccurate cuts. Cutting smaller sheets produces better results.

🚫 Banned materials — DO NOT BRING

These materials produce toxic fumes, damage the machine, or create fire hazards. Any attempt to cut banned materials will result in immediate loss of laser privileges.
  • PVC / vinyl / any chlorine-based material
  • Polycarbonate (Lexan)
  • Rubber (all forms)
  • Fibreglass / coated carbon fibre
  • MDF or LDF (toxic glue fumes)
  • Foam core / polystyrene foam
  • Galvanised or mirrored metal
  • HDPE (milk bottle plastic)
  • Pressure-treated wood
  • Casting resins
  • Felt

Not sure? Ask Tech Support before your session — do not bring untested materials on the day.

Step 4 — Securing lightweight materials

Lightweight materials (paper, card, leather, film) can be lifted by the exhaust system, causing incorrect cuts or fire. You MUST secure them before cutting.

Options:

  • Tape the edges to the rulers on the laser bed (bring your own tape)
  • Glue to a heavier board — good for leather and thin plastic
  • Design tabs into your vector file so cut pieces stay connected until you remove them
All jobs must be watched for the full duration of the session. Do not leave the machine unattended.

Step 5 — Share your file before your session

Upload your finished file to Google Drive or WeTransfer and paste the link into your booking request. This lets Tech Support check your file before you arrive — saving time and avoiding re-cuts on the day.

Accepted formats: SVG, AI — not JPEG, PNG or PDF.

💡 If this is your first time using the laser, tick the "First time" box in your request. A short test cut will be done before your main job to confirm settings for your material.

Quick checklist before you submit

  • File is SVG or AI (not JPEG/PNG/PDF)
  • Document colour mode is RGB
  • Vector lines are 0.001–0.005pt stroke, no fill
  • Cut lines are Red (255,0,0), Engrave lines Blue (0,0,255)
  • Material is on the approved list and is ≤12mm thick
  • File link shared in your booking request
  • You will be present for the full session
