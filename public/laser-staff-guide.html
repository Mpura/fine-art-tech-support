⚡ Laser Operator Reference

Fine Art Tech Support — Staff only · SA Argus 80W CO₂ Laser · Bed: 1000mm × 600mm

🔒 STAFF USE ONLY — not for students
This guide covers cutting and engraving profiles, pulse settings, startup procedure, and safety. The student guide covers file prep only.

Machine Startup Procedure

1
Switch on the laser using the key switch on the control panel.
2
Press the Motors button — the machine will home automatically.
3
Check water cooler — ensure water is flowing and temperature is 20–30°C. Do not run the machine if water temperature exceeds 33°C.
4
Turn on Air Assist and Exhaust — air must be on for all cutting. Keep it on even at low level to protect the lens.
5
Open VectorDRAW → Processing Path → Select USB Device to connect the PC to the laser (do this every time VectorDRAW starts).
6
Import student's .AI file, set up paths, assign templates, set processing order (engrave before cut).
7
Send to machine — file names are max 8 characters.
8
Place material on bed, use focus tool to set correct height, zero the position on hand control (bottom-left default).
9
Switch on Laser button, set to Auto function, and run the job.
10
When done: turn off laser, air, exhaust, and send head to Home position.
💡 For thick materials (>9mm), lower the focus by moving the nozzle closer to the material. Ensure the nozzle does not touch the material — this restricts airflow and contaminates the lens.

Step options

Step = Y-axis distance between scan lines. Available options: 0.2mm · 0.5mm · 0.8mm (in VectorDRAW). Smaller step = finer result, slower job. Use 0.07–0.14mm for fine detail; 0.1–0.14mm for time-saving passes.

Pulse Frequency Guide

Pulse frequency controls how the laser fires during slow cuts. Applies to slow mode only — pulse is automatic in fast mode.

Pulse rangeWhen to use
1,000–5,000 HzWood, MDF, foam, rubber — materials prone to burning. Lower pulse reduces char.
15,000 HzGeneral wood and MDF cutting profiles.
30,000 HzRowMark and similar engraving panels.
50,000 HzAcrylic (cast) — high pulse generates heat that fuses edges smoothly. Reduce for frosted/matte edge effect.

VectorDRAW Path Settings Reference

Slow Mode (Cut)
  • Speed: 0.1–200 mm/s
  • Power: 1–95% (Start Power: 100%)
  • Pulse: 1,000–50,000 Hz
  • Start Power: power as beam enters material
  • Delay Laser: pause before cutting — useful for thick wood to allow beam to penetrate
  • Min Power: applies at corners when head slows (fast mode only)
Fast Mode (Cut/Score)
  • Speed: above 35 mm/s
  • Start Power: always 0%
  • Min Power: ~50% (half of Power value)
  • Pulse: automatic
  • Use for: paper, card, cloth, surface scoring
Sweep / Engrave
  • Speed: 100–800 mm/s
  • Power: 1–95%
  • Step: 0.2 / 0.5 / 0.8mm
  • Bi-directional: 2× faster
  • Uni-directional: more accurate
  • Always process before cutting
Contour / Line options
  • Outer / Inner / Online
  • Offset: set distance from artwork
  • Close cut line: overlaps start/end for smoother entry
  • Processing order: top = first
  • Max 16 templates saved

Rotary Attachment

  • Clamps numbered 1, 2, 3 — slide in anticlockwise, lock clockwise
  • Lower the bed to fit the rotary; zero on the top sharp edge of the cone
  • Rotate artwork 90° in VectorDRAW to align with rotary axis
  • Y-pulse formula: 51.5 ÷ diameter (mm) × 200
  • Switch hand control to Rotary mode before sending
  • Glass engraving: use 80gsm copy paper wet with Windowlene on the engraving area — do not use air assist with this method
  • After use: switch hand control back from Rotary mode and reset Y-pulse to 200

Water Cooling & Maintenance

Never run the laser if water temperature exceeds 33°C. Absolute maximum is 35°C — exceeding this will damage the laser tube.
  • Water type: Distilled water only (available at Midas)
  • Operating temperature: 20–30°C (min 12°C, max 33°C)
  • Water level: Check weekly
  • Replace water: At least once a month to prevent algae build-up
  • Alarm test: Squeeze the water pipe — alarm should sound. If it doesn't, contact a technician before using the machine.

Maintenance schedule

  • After every session: Wipe motion arm/rail with soft cloth. Clean slats if charred (use Hand Cleaner / Handy Andy). Dirty slats increase flashback and fire risk.
  • Lens: Spray with cleaning solvent, wipe in quarters then halves then full circles with lens paper.
  • Mirrors: Clean with lens paper in circular motions using cleaning solution.
  • Rails: Grease rails and grease nipples regularly. Lubricate rotary after every use.
  • Bed levelling: Check all 4 corners in cross-over fashion.
  • USB for file transfer: Must be under 1 GB, formatted as FAT (not FAT32 or NTFS).

Safety Checklist

Never leave the machine unattended while it is running. You must be present for the entire job.
  • Air assist on full capacity for all cutting jobs
  • Exhaust on before starting
  • Water cooler running and within temperature range
  • No banned materials — if in doubt, refuse and ask student to verify material
  • Lightweight materials must be secured before cutting
  • Do not exceed 95% power
  • Run a test cut for first-time students and new materials before the full job
  • Keep fire extinguisher accessible
  • Emergency stop button location: right side of control panel

Banned materials (never cut or engrave)

  • PVC / vinyl / any chlorine-based material — produces toxic chlorine gas
  • Polycarbonate (Lexan) — produces toxic fumes, doesn't cut cleanly
  • Fibreglass / coated carbon fibre — toxic particulate
  • Foam core / polystyrene foam — fire risk, toxic fumes
  • Galvanised or mirrored metal — toxic zinc fumes
  • HDPE (milk bottle plastic) — melts and clogs, fire risk
  • Pressure-treated wood — toxic chemical fumes
  • Casting resins — toxic fumes
  • Felt — fire risk, inconsistent results
